Kart racing on the real streets of Bulli

Race round a 2.7 km loop of Bulli on the NSW South Coast. Drift for mini-turbos, dodge the roos and the wheelie bins, smash your kart to bits, and race up to eight players online.

Free. No ads, no in-app purchases.

Small town. Big race.

Everything you'd see on a drive round Bulli is in the race, and most of it is in your way.

Drift, boost, launch

Drift through the bends for mini-turbos, hit the boost pads and fly off the ramps.

Throw the local stuff

Pinecones, boomerangs, seaweed on the road, and a magpie you can send after the leader.

Mind the locals

Kangaroos crossing Blackall Street, wheelie bins on bin night, cockatoos on lap 2 and magpies swooping in spring.

Smash 'em up

Hit hard enough and your kart flies to bits in a cartoon puff, then pops back together on the track.

Race real people

Up to eight racers online on the Sydney server, with AI filling the grid, a leaderboard and a lap record.

Your driver, your kart

Sunny the cockatoo, Gala the galah, Kooka the kookaburra or Lori the lorikeet, in the kart and colours you pick.

On the track

Touch controls with auto-accelerate on phones, or plug in a controller.

Bulli, NSW

A real place, one lap at a time

The track follows real streets, rebuilt from the map, and Bulli's real sky: live time of day and weather, with rain making the roads slippery.

  • Up Trinity Row and Blackall Street, past the beach
  • Round the Sandon Point headland and its hairpin
  • Back through the neighbourhood's streets, paths and parks
